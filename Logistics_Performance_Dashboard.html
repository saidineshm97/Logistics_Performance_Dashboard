Key Insights

  • BlueDart Freight is the most reliable carrier (89.6% on-time), while Trident Movers lags at 76.0% despite similar shipment volume — worth reviewing whether Trident's route assignments skew toward harder lanes or if this reflects genuine service-quality gaps.
  • Air is both the fastest and most reliable mode (87.7% on-time, 3.6-day average transit) but costs nearly 3x more per km than Road and 5x more than Rail — a clear speed-vs-cost tradeoff for time-sensitive shipments.
  • Carrier capacity shortages and traffic congestion are the top two delay drivers (18.8% and 18.2% of all delays), suggesting demand-forecasting and route-planning improvements would have the biggest impact on on-time rate.
  • Chennai Hub has the lowest on-time rate (79.2%) among all five origin hubs despite handling a mid-sized volume — a candidate for a focused operational review.
